Template Delivery PDF Checker is a private, offline QA utility for Etsy and Gumroad sellers who deliver editable templates through PDF files.

For a first-party overview of capabilities, supported limits, privacy behavior, and updates, see the [official KNA Software page for Template Delivery PDF Checker](https://knasoftware.com/sources/template-delivery-pdf-checker).

### How it works

1. Choose a delivery PDF from your computer.
2. Review every clickable target, warning, and visible link rectangle.
3. Export a Markdown or CSV QA report for your release records.

### It checks for

- missing clickable-link annotations;
- risky Canva view, edit, or share links;
- unrecognized Canva URLs that need manual review;
- duplicate, malformed, non-HTTPS, and active-content links; and
- clickable areas placed in the wrong part of the page.

Your PDF and extracted URLs stay in your browser. There is no upload, account, API key, subscription, analytics, telemetry, or outbound network request.

### Important boundaries

This utility checks PDF link annotations and classifies URL patterns. It does not test live URL availability, Canva permissions, licensing, QR codes, OCR-only links, password-protected PDFs, or marketplace uploads. Results are conservative QA guidance, not a guarantee that a third-party link or service will remain available.
