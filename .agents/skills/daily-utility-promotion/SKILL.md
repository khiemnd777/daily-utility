# Daily Utility Promotion

## Entry

Verify the product is `PUBLISHED`, both approved sales pages and the KNA Software catalog page are live, and the proposed claims match the product, acceptance evidence, shared price, support scope, and guarantee. Promotion cannot repair an incomplete release.

Begin target research and tailored drafting automatically after `PUBLISHED`; do not wait for another reminder. Release approval does not authorize an external post.

## Reuse the durable record first

Read `factory/promotion/channels.json` and the product's `marketing/promotion-log.json` before searching the web or opening community discovery surfaces. If the product log does not exist, treat that as no recorded prior use—not proof that no outreach occurred—and create the log when recording the first approved attempt.

- Treat a `published` or `pending` entry for the same product and channel as already used. Do not submit the product there again unless the user approves a clearly identified follow-up after the prior state changes.
- Shortlist existing channels whose `product_fit` matches the product and whose `reuse_policy` allows reuse. Registry inclusion is not posting permission.
- Recheck only the shortlisted channels' current activity, visible rules, link policy, account access, and moderation flow. Stop using any channel whose current evidence conflicts with the registry and update the record.
- Research net-new channels only for audience gaps that the eligible registry entries do not cover. Do not spend time rediscovering known channels.

## Research new targets

Use current web research to find communities, threads, newsletters, directories, or articles where the target buyer is already discussing the recurring problem. For each candidate, inspect recent activity, audience fit, self-promotion rules, allowed link types, and whether the seller account can post legitimately.

Return one ranked shortlist that distinguishes reusable registry channels from net-new candidates. Each target should include its URL, audience, evidence of fit, relevant posting rule, risk of removal, prior-use state for this product, and a tailored content angle.

## Prepare content

- Lead with the buyer problem and a useful observation, not generic product hype.
- State what the utility does, who it is for, what it does not do, and the shared price when relevant. Use one reviewed call to action per draft: the KNA Software page or one verified sales channel chosen for the destination. Do not imply channel-exclusive pricing or availability unless the publication evidence supports it.
- Disclose seller affiliation. Do not fabricate usage numbers, reviews, urgency, discounts, or guarantees.
- Adapt the copy to each community; do not cross-post identical text in rapid succession.

## Approval and posting

Research and drafting are non-posting work. Before any external post, comment, message, or submission, show the exact destination, account context, and final copy and obtain action-time approval for that item or clearly bounded batch.

After approval, follow the destination's visible rules, post once, and verify the resulting URL or visible success state. Stop on moderation warnings, CAPTCHA, account restrictions, unclear rules, or material form changes. Never bypass safeguards, send unsolicited direct messages, or manufacture engagement.

After each approved attempt, update the product promotion log with `published`, `pending`, `rejected`, or `skipped`, the destination and public post URL when available, verified timestamps, account context, and concise evidence. Add genuinely new reusable destinations to the shared channel registry with current fit, rules, access, and verification facts. Never store credentials, cookies, or private account data.

Report posted URLs and timestamps, pending submissions, rejected or skipped targets with reasons, and any follow-up that would require new authorization. Do not promise ongoing monitoring unless a separate automation is explicitly requested.
